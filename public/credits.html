ojjyProxy is a standalone project by Jonas Lee, matching ojjy’s game hub blue. It combines four pinned Scramjet releases and Ultraviolet with an operator-owned Wisp relay.

Primary integration reference: Proxy2, shared by The Fool, who also helped with the UI. Engines and transports: Mercury Workshop and Titanium Network.
